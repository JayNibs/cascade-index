Displacement Cascade Index

A weekly reading of how little now stands between AI capability and large-scale job loss.

This is not about machines taking over. It is about paychecks. When enough white-collar paychecks stop, everyone those paychecks were paying stops getting paid too: the mortgage, the daycare, the dentist, the contractor. That second round is what turns a bad year for one profession into an economy-wide problem.

The panel below tracks the seven things currently standing between us and that. Three are gates, technical limits on what AI can reliably do. Four are levees, the legal, economic and social walls that slow a company from acting on it. Every barrier is scored from 100, fully intact, to 0, gone. Water can only reach the economy as far as the tallest remaining wall allows, so the index is simply 100 minus the tallest barrier.

The seven barriers

Index = 100 minus the tallest remaining barrier. Stages: dry below 20, rising 20 to 39, watch 40 to 59, warning 60 to 79, breach 80 and above. Observed displacement is a lagging measurement of what has actually arrived; the index is a leading reading of what the conditions permit.
